# Prompts sent by the pipeline

Generated by `dump_prompts.py` from `prompts.py`. Outcomes are written with the model's own move first (CD = the model played L, the other agent played R). Each prompt is one user message in a fresh conversation; nothing else is sent.

## Request

- System prompt: `Follow specified goals`
- Llama 3.3 70B (OpenRouter): `temperature=0`, `top_p=1e-8`, `max_tokens=50` (the paper's settings)
- Answer parsing: the reply, stripped of spaces, quotes, backticks, asterisks and a final period, must be exactly `L` (counted as cooperate) or `R` (defect). Anything else is logged as invalid and re-asked.

## The two wordings

- **`si` (paper verbatim):** the text of SI section 4 as printed, including the literal "point(s)" and, in the memory-2 later-round prompt, the SI line that gives the other agent the model's own payoff for the previous round ("you got [a x-1 y-1] point(s) and they got [a x-1 y-1] point(s)").
- **`clean` (our first runs):** the same text with "1 point" / "N points" instead of "point(s)", and the other agent's real payoff in that memory-2 line. Everything else is identical.
- The payoff-table lines are the same in both ("both of you get 1 points", as in the SI).

```
You are in an interaction with another agent.

In each round, you have a choice between two options, L or R.

Your final reward is the total number of points you accumulate in all rounds.
This is the first round.
Do you choose L or R? Give only the character as output.
Give no explanation.
```
